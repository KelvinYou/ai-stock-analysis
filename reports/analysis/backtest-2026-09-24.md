# Backtest comparison — 2026-09-24

> [Status: Warning] Historical development research only. The selected universe and rule have been inspected in prior sessions. A historical point-estimate win is not an independently validated edge.

## Protocol

- Data: checked-in local price histories ending 2026-09-17; synchronized open and close bars, with dividends and total returns not verified.
- Rule: at each month end rank trailing 12-month close returns, buy the top three equally at the next common session open, and rebalance monthly. Long only, unlevered.
- Benchmark: buy all names equally at the same first entry and hold to the same final exit. Matched passive is a second diagnostic with the candidate's rebalance schedule.
- Costs: assumed 10 bps per side on actual turnover, including final liquidation. A 20 bps per side full-period stress was also run.
- This deterministic allocator is separate from the four-agent AI pipeline.

## Verdict

- [Status: Warning] The eleven-name **price-only allocator** achieved an observed historical point-estimate win over long hold. It has not demonstrated a robust excess-return interval or a clean prospective holdout; do not promote it for a real trade on this result.
- [Status: Critical] The **AI overall signal** did not beat long hold and fails the repo's numerical promotion gate. The allocator win does not validate AI prediction skill.
- Stopping the search at this point avoids further selection on the same inspected history. The next meaningful test is a frozen, prospective comparison with point-in-time universe/total returns and calibrated costs.
