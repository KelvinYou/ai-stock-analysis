Operational Performance Data Footnote




Current quarter payments volume and other select metrics are provided in the operational performance data supplement to provide more
recent operating data. Service revenue continues to be recognized based on payments volume in the prior quarter.

Total transactions represent payments and cash transactions as reported by Visa clients on their operating certificates. Processed
transactions include payments and cash transactions, and represent transactions using cards and other form factors carrying the Visa, Visa
Electron, V PAY, Interlink and PLUS brands processed on Visa’s networks.

Reported volume, transaction and card information may be updated to reflect revised client submissions or other adjustments. Prior-period
updates are not material. Figures may not recalculate exactly due to rounding. Percentage changes and totals are calculated based on
unrounded numbers. Constant-dollar growth rates exclude the impact of foreign currency fluctuations against the U.S. dollar in measuring
performance.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures




                              We present our results and our financial outlook on a non-GAAP basis, which exclude certain items that we believe are not representative of our continuing
                              operations, as they may be non-recurring or have no cash impact, and may distort our longer term operating trends.
